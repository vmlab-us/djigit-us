MINI financing assistance

Financing may be preferable for high mileage, personalization, or long-term ownership. Include interest, insurance, premium fuel where required, tires, service, parking, electricity for an EV, charging equipment, and post-warranty repairs in the plan.

A MINI financing worksheet should show selling price, down payment, annual percentage rate, term, taxes, fees, optional products, trade equity, amount financed, and total of payments. A longer loan can lower the monthly amount while increasing interest and delaying equity. DJIGIT US keeps the payment connected to the full transaction.

Lenders—not DJIGIT US—make MINI credit decisions. Credit history, income, existing debt, residence, down payment, vehicle, loan-to-value ratio, and supporting documents may affect the result. Customers can use our credit application when ready without accepting a specific vehicle or proposal.

Buying a new MINI

On a new MINI, confirm body style, powertrain, drivetrain, wheel size, roof configuration, seat and cargo arrangement, driver-assistance content, packages, and charging equipment where relevant. The visual configurator can encourage options quickly, so separate functional requirements from colors and decorative choices before finalizing a build.

Request a written MINI breakdown covering vehicle price, factory and retailer-installed options, taxes, registration, documented fees, trade credit, cash due, amount financed or lease structure, and optional products. Current programs and inventory change; an advertised example does not establish the price or approval for another vehicle or customer.

Buying a used MINI

A used MINI should be checked for complete service history, oil or coolant concerns, tires and brakes, suspension noise, electronics, roof and window seals, convertible-top operation where applicable, prior modifications, body repair, and title status. Electric candidates add battery, charging, cable, software, and recall checks.

DJIGIT US can organize the used MINI search around maximum price, acceptable years and mileage, must-have equipment, condition, financing, and timing. Used inventory moves quickly, but speed should not replace verification of VIN, history, title, condition, recall status, documents, independent inspection, and actual availability.

Trade-in support for your next MINI

For a MINI trade, identify body style, engine or electric variant, packages, mileage, payoff, keys, records, tire condition, modifications, and any roof or interior wear. Keep the appraisal separate from replacement pricing. Negative equity does not disappear when rolled into a new agreement, so payoff, appraisal, taxes, cash due, and replacement-vehicle price should remain visible as separate numbers.
