Buying a new versus used Tucson

For a new Tucson, confirm trim, front- or all-wheel drive, safety technology, wheel size, seating materials, warranty terms, and installed accessories. For a used Tucson, review maintenance, recall status, tires, electronics, prior damage, keys, and warranty eligibility rather than assuming every example is equivalent.

Check recall completion, service history, tire wear, electronic displays, driver-assistance operation, body repairs, title records, and all supplied keys. A vehicle-history report is useful but cannot replace a physical inspection, document review, and test drive appropriate to the compact SUV. Confirm that recalls and service campaigns have been addressed where applicable.
