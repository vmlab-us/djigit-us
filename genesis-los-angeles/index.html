Trade-in support for your next Genesis

For a Genesis trade, trim and factory options can materially affect comparisons. Provide the VIN, payoff, mileage, keys, records, condition details, and accurate photos, then keep appraisal, discount, and financing figures separate. Negative equity does not disappear when rolled into a new agreement, so payoff, appraisal, taxes, cash due, and replacement-vehicle price should remain visible as separate numbers.
